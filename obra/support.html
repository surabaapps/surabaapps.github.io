Obra Support

Obra helps contractors log daily job-site progress — photos, voice memos, and notes — and turn it into a client-ready daily report you can share over WhatsApp or email.

Contact

Email obra.app.support@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. Please include your device, app version if you know it, and a short description of what you expected to happen.

Common questions

  • How do I create a daily report? Open a project, capture photos and voice memos or type notes for the day, then tap Daily update. You can edit the text before sharing.
  • How do I send it to my client? On the report card, tap Share. The write-up and that day's photos go out together through the app you pick (Messages, WhatsApp, Mail, and so on).
  • How do I delete my account? In the app, go to Settings → Delete Account. See Delete account and data.
