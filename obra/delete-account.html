Delete your Obra account and data

App: Obra for iOS (ai.ashish.obra)
Developer: Ashish Patel

From the app

  1. Sign in to Obra.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. This cannot be undone.

This permanently deletes your Obra account and all associated projects, photos, voice memos, notes, and reports.

Logging out or uninstalling the app does not delete your account or cloud data.

If you cannot open the app

Email obra.app.support@gmail.com from the Apple or Google account you use with Obra and ask for account deletion.

What is deleted

  • Your Obra Auth account
  • All projects, photos, voice memos, notes, and reports

What is kept

  • Your Apple or Google account itself is not deleted.
  • Device or iCloud backups may still contain app data. Manage those in your Apple account settings.

Questions

Email obra.app.support@gmail.com.
