Suraba Apps · EverDue

Delete your EverDue data

EverDue has no user accounts and does not store your data on an EverDue server. Everything lives on your device.

Delete one item

  1. Open EverDue.
  2. Open the item.
  3. Delete it. That also removes its saved document and scheduled reminders.

Delete all EverDue data on this device

  1. Delete the EverDue app.
  2. App data is removed from the device, subject to your iPhone or iPad backup and restore settings.

If you restore a backup that included EverDue, local data from that backup may return.
