EverDue Support

EverDue is a local-only iOS app for household renewals, bills, and documents. There is no account to recover.

Contact

Email everdue.app.support@gmail.com.

Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what you expected to happen.

Common questions

  • Where is my data? On this device only. EverDue does not use an EverDue cloud account.
  • How do I add an item? Scan a document, import a photo or PDF, share a file into EverDue, or enter the details yourself.
  • Reminders are not appearing. Enable notifications for EverDue in iOS Settings, then confirm the item has a due date.
  • How do I delete data? Delete an item in the app, or uninstall EverDue. See Delete your data.
