EverDue Support

EverDue is an iOS app for household renewals, bills, and documents. You can use it without an account, or sign in with Apple or Google so reminder details can restore after you reinstall. Scanned documents stay on this device.

Contact

Email everdue.app.support@gmail.com.

Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what you expected to happen.

Common questions

  • Where is my data? Documents stay on this device. If you sign in, reminder fields also sync to your EverDue account so they can restore after reinstall.
  • How do I add an item? Scan a document, import a photo or PDF, share a file into EverDue, or enter the details yourself.
  • Reminders are not appearing. Enable notifications for EverDue in iOS Settings, then confirm the item has a due date.
  • How do I delete an account? Open EverDue, tap Account, then tap Delete account. See Delete your data.
  • How do I delete data? Delete an item in the app, delete your account if you signed in, or uninstall EverDue. See Delete your data.
