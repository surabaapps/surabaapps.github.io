Mango Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026
Apps: Mango for Android (com.farmlog.mango) and Mango for iOS (com.farmlog.Mango)
Developer: Ashish Patel

This policy describes how the Mango apps handle information on Android and iOS.

Summary

Mango is a farm-management app. It does not sell your data and does not share your farm records with other Mango users. How sign-in and storage work depends on the platform, as described below.

Information we collect

  • Account information — on Android, name and email from Google Sign-In; on iOS, the phone number and name you use to sign in.
  • Profile name — the name you set in the app.
  • Farm records you enter — farm and crop names, activity, wages, payments, balances, notes, and optional worker or partner phone numbers.
  • Voice input — if you tap the microphone, the device converts speech to help enter an amount. Audio is not stored on Mango servers.

Android

  • You sign in with Google. Your farms, people, and money records are stored in your private Mango workspace hosted by Supabase (Postgres) in the Asia (Mumbai) region, protected by row-level security so only your signed-in account can read or change them.
  • The app needs internet access to sign in and to save or load records.
  • Optional usage analytics and crash diagnostics may be sent to Firebase. Farm names, person names, money amounts, notes, and tokens are never included. You can turn this off in You → Privacy.
  • Android backup may include app data depending on your device settings.

iOS

  • You sign in with a phone number. Farm records are stored on the device.
  • iCloud or device backup may include app data depending on your device settings.

Sharing

Mango does not sell personal or financial data. On Android, Google (sign-in, optional Firebase) and Supabase act as processors to provide the app. We do not use advertising identifiers.

Permissions

  • Internet — required on Android for sign-in and cloud storage.
  • Microphone — optional on both platforms, only when you choose voice entry.

Children

Mango is intended for adults managing farm operations and is not directed at children under 18.

Deleting your data

See Delete account and data. Uninstalling the app without deleting a cloud account leaves server-held records in place until you delete the account.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when that happens.
