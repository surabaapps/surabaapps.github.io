Mango Support

Mango is a farm-management app for Android and iPhone. On iPhone you sign in with Apple or Google. On Android you sign in with Google. Your farms, people, and money records stay in your private Mango workspace.

Contact

Email contactfarmhand@gmail.com.

Please include your device (Android or iPhone), app version if you know it, and a short description of what you expected to happen.

Common questions

  • How do I sign in? On iPhone, use Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. On Android, use Sign in with Google. Use the same method next time to see your farms.
  • Where is my data? In your private Mango cloud workspace. You need an internet connection to sign in, save, and load records.
  • I reinstalled the app. Sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used before to load your workspace.
  • How do I delete my account? Open You → Delete account and data and confirm. See Delete account and data.
  • Can I turn off analytics? Open You → Privacy. Farm, person, and money details are never included.
