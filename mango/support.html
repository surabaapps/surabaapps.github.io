Mangobook Support

Mangobook is a farm-management app. You sign in with Apple or Google. Your farms, people, and money records stay in your private Mangobook workspace.

Contact

Email contactfarmhand@gmail.com.

Please include your device, app version if you know it, and a short description of what you expected to happen.

Common questions

  • How do I sign in? Use Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. Use the same method next time to see your farms.
  • Where is my data? In your private Mangobook cloud workspace. You need an internet connection to sign in, save, and load records.
  • I reinstalled the app. Sign in with the same Apple or Google account you used before to load your workspace.
  • How do I delete my account? Open You → Delete account and data and confirm. See Delete account and data.
  • Can I turn off analytics? Open You → Privacy. Farm, person, and money details are never included.
