Suraba Apps · Mango

Delete your Mango account and data

Apps: Mango for Android (com.farmlog.mango) and Mango for iOS (com.farmlog.Mango)
Developer: Ashish Patel

Android and iPhone use the same Mango cloud workspace. Deleting your account from either app removes that workspace.

From the app

  1. Sign in with the Google account you use for Mango.
  2. Open You.
  3. Tap Delete account and data and confirm.

This deletes your Mango Auth user and the farms, crops, people, and activity stored for that account. It cannot be undone.

Logging out or uninstalling the app does not delete your cloud workspace.

If you cannot open the app

Email contactfarmhand@gmail.com from the Google account used to sign in and ask for account deletion. We will delete the matching Mango account and workspace records.

What is deleted

  • Your Mango Auth account
  • All farms, crops, and crop seasons
  • All activity, wage, and payment records
  • Worker and partner contact details you entered
  • All balances and profit-share settings

What is kept

  • Your Google account itself is not deleted.
  • Device, iCloud, or Android backups may still contain app data. Manage those in your Google or Apple account settings.

After deletion

Cloud deletion is permanent. Signing in again with the same Google account creates a new empty workspace.
