Delete your Mango account and data

Apps: Mango for Android (com.farmlog.mango) and Mango for iOS (com.farmlog.Mango)
Developer: Ashish Patel

How deletion works depends on the platform, because Android currently uses a cloud workspace and iOS currently stores records on the device.

Android

  1. Sign in with the Google account you use for Mango.
  2. Open You.
  3. Tap Delete account and data and confirm.

This deletes your Mango Auth user and the farms, crops, people, and activity stored for that account. It cannot be undone.

iOS

  1. Delete the Mango app from the iPhone, or
  2. Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Mango → Delete App.

Logging out does not erase on-device records. Uninstalling removes the local workspace.

If you cannot open the app

Email contactfarmhand@gmail.com from the Google or phone account used to sign in and ask for account deletion. We will delete any matching Mango cloud records.

What is deleted (Android cloud workspace)

  • Your Mango Auth account
  • All farms, crops, and crop seasons
  • All activity, wage, and payment records
  • Worker and partner contact details you entered
  • All balances and profit-share settings

What is kept

  • Your Google or Apple ID itself is not deleted.
  • Device or iCloud backups may still contain app data. Manage those in your Google or Apple account settings.

After deletion

Cloud deletion is permanent. Signing in again on Android creates a new empty workspace.
